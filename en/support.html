Frequently asked questions

How do I recover my game if I change phones or lose it?

VIDA RPG has no accounts and no cloud copy: your game lives only on your device. The only way to keep it is to make a copy yourself: More → Settings → Export creates a file you can save wherever you like (your own cloud, an email to yourself, another device). On the new phone, Import reads that file and replaces the current game.

If you uninstalled the app without exporting, the game cannot be recovered: nobody has a copy. The PRO subscription, if you had one, can be recovered with "Restore purchases" on the subscription screen, because it belongs to your store account.

How do I change the language?

In More → Settings → Language. You can choose Spanish, English or Polish regardless of the system language. All three languages are bundled in the app, so no connection is needed. Anything you wrote yourself (names, goals, texts) is not translated: it is yours and is kept as is.

What happens if my character dies?

Your character has 1000 health points. The vices you log take health away; if it reaches zero, the character dies. While dead, it earns no experience and you cannot log habits. The only way back is to complete the penalty you set yourself (the default is covering 50 km in under 24 hours; you can change it from that same screen). Once completed, the character revives with half its health and the game continues: you do not lose your level, your Baros or your missions.

How does the streak work, and what protects it?

The streak counts consecutive days on which you log at least one positive action. A blank day breaks it, unless you carry a shield: they are bought in the Shop with Baros, you can carry two at most, and each one covers one blank day and is used up. A shield keeps the days you already had; it does not grant new ones.

If a streak of three days or more breaks, a 24-hour recovery window opens: if you log six positive actions within it, you get the whole streak back. Milestones (7, 30, 100, 180 and 365 days) unlock exclusive cosmetics. Days are counted in universal time (UTC), so the day change may not match midnight in your time zone exactly.

What exactly does the AI Coach do?

It is an optional feature that analyses your goals and progress and proposes concrete adjustments or actions, in your language. It only acts when you tap its button, in three places: when refining a goal, when requesting the weekly analysis in Progress, or when asking for a suggestion about a mission. At that moment a structured summary of your goals and progress is sent to Google's artificial-intelligence services through Firebase: without your name, without photographs and without the content of your golden card. It never sends anything on its own.

It has a limit of ten queries per day (one hundred with PRO). You can switch it off completely from Settings; the rest of the app works exactly the same without it. Its suggestions are ideas to weigh up, not instructions or professional advice. More detail in the Privacy Policy.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. The whole game works offline. Only the AI Coach, when you invoke it, and the PRO subscription screen, when you open it, need one.

What is PRO and how do I cancel it?

PRO is an optional subscription whose only benefit is raising the AI Coach limit from ten to one hundred queries per day. It unlocks nothing else, and there are no ads to remove. It is bought and cancelled from Google Play (or the App Store), like any other subscription; the app cannot cancel it for you and puts no obstacles in your way.

How do I delete all my data?

With the "Reset the game" button in Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Nothing remains on any server. The steps are in the Data Deletion Policy.
