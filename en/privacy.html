Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026 · app version 1.9.0

VIDA RPG is an app with no user accounts and no sign-up. Everything you record stays on your device, and the whole app works offline. This policy explains plainly what is stored, where, and what happens in the only two optional features that talk to the internet.

1. Summary

  • There are no accounts, no registration and no passwords. We never ask for your real name, email or phone number.
  • Your entire game is stored exclusively on your device. There is no developer server, no remote database and no VIDA RPG cloud copy.
  • There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics and no trackers.
  • The camera and photo gallery are used only if you choose to create your avatar from a photo, and the photo is never stored or sent.
  • Notifications are local and optional.
  • The AI Coach is optional and only sends anything when you tap its button. It can be switched off completely.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data. The developer holds no copy of anything.

2. What data the app stores

Only your game data, and only on your device:

  • The name you give your character and the look of its avatar.
  • Your active life areas and their experience.
  • The habits and vices you log.
  • The missions, plans and goals you create, including any text you write yourself.
  • Baros, experience, level, streak, shields, milestones, records, challenges, achievements and action history.
  • The content of your golden card.
  • Your preferences: language, currency symbol, sound, music, vibration, visual effects, game mode and reminders.
  • The mentors you have unlocked and the lessons you have read.
  • If you created your avatar from a photo: the resulting pixel grid, never the photo (see section 5).
  • For the AI Coach: whether you switched it off, how many queries you made today and in total, which suggestions you accepted and, since 1.9.0, how many tokens those queries cost. These are counters, not conversations.
  • If you subscribed to PRO: whether the subscription is active, which plan, and until when. Nothing else.

No identifying data is collected: no real name, no email, no phone number, no device identifiers, no location. The country code the AI Coach uses is not stored either: it is read from your system settings at the moment of the query and never written to your save file (section 8).

3. Where it is stored and who can see it

Exclusively in the app's private storage on your device, through the system preferences API (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS). It is not sent to any server. The developer cannot see it, does not receive it and has no way to access it.

On Android, system backup is disabled for this app, so your data does not leave the device that way either. On iPhone, Apple does not let an app exclude itself from iCloud backup: if you have it enabled, your game is part of your own device's backup, encrypted and inside your Apple account, under Apple's privacy policy. You can exclude VIDA RPG from your phone's iCloud settings.

4. What the app does not do

  • It does not create user accounts or profiles.
  • It shows no ads, and never will: that is a product principle, not a temporary situation.
  • It contains no advertising trackers and no third-party analytics tools. No cookies, no profiling.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.
  • It does not use the microphone, location, contacts or cross-app tracking.

5. Camera, gallery and the photo avatar

You can create your character's avatar from a photograph. The camera and the gallery are used only at that moment and for nothing else:

  • The system asks for the camera or photo permission when you choose the option. You can deny it: you simply will not be able to use this feature, and the rest of the app works the same.
  • The image is opened in memory so you can frame it, and it is converted, right then and inside your device, into a 20 × 20 pixel grid with a palette of about 14 colours. No external service, no facial recognition, no biometrics and no artificial intelligence are involved.
  • The photograph is never stored or transmitted. It is not copied to any app folder, not saved to the gallery, not uploaded to any server and not shared with anyone. As soon as the avatar is generated, it is released from memory.
  • The only thing kept in your game is that grid: a list of colour indexes of less than two kilobytes, from which the original photo cannot be reconstructed.
  • You can switch back to the classic avatar at any time and erase the grid by resetting the game.

6. Notifications

Reminders are local notifications: your own phone generates them from your game, offline and without any server. They are optional: you can turn them on or off in Settings and in the system's notification settings. The app does not know whether you read them and sends nothing to anyone about them.

7. Exporting and importing your game

From Settings you can export your game to a file (including, if you created it, the avatar grid, but never a photograph). The app opens the system share sheet so that you choose where to save it or whom to send it to. That file is under your control and your responsibility: the app never sends it anywhere on its own. The import option reads a file you select and replaces the current game.

8. The AI Coach

The AI Coach is an optional feature that analyses your goals and progress and proposes concrete adjustments and actions. The entire app works the same without it.

When something is sent

Only when you explicitly invoke it by tapping its button, in one of three places: when refining a goal, when requesting the weekly analysis in Progress, or when asking for a suggestion about a specific mission. The app never sends anything on its own: not in the background, not on launch, not on close.

Where it goes

To Google's artificial-intelligence services, through Firebase (Firebase AI Logic, which queries the Gemini model). No developer server is involved. Google's handling of that data is governed by the Firebase and Gemini API terms.

What is sent

A structured summary of what you are asking about: the goal with its figure, date and committed hours; your experience per day over the last two weeks; how many habits you completed in each area; your streaks; the vices you logged; your active missions with their progress; and the language it should answer in.

Since version 1.9.0 it also sends your device’s country code: two letters, such as ES, GB or PL. It serves one purpose only: if a sign of harm to yourself or to others appears in what you are asking about, the coach has to be able to point you to the right emergency resource. Language is not enough — someone using the app in English may be in any country — and the wrong emergency number is worse than none. That code comes from the regional settings you already have in your system, the same ones that decide how dates are shown to you: it is not geolocation, it does not use GPS and it requires no permission. It is not stored anywhere: it is read at the moment of the query. If it cannot be determined, nothing is sent, and the coach gives 112 as the European emergency number and also refers you to your local one.

What is never sent

  • Your real name, your character's name, or any identifying data.
  • No photograph, and not your avatar's pixel grid either.
  • The content of your golden card.
  • No calendar dates: days travel as "three days ago".
  • No other location data: not your time zone, not your city, not coordinates, nothing of the sort. Only the two-letter country code explained above.

This is verified by an automated test suite that inspects every request and fails if any of that data slips in.

How to switch it off

From Settings you can switch the AI Coach off completely. All its entry points disappear and absolutely nothing is sent. While it is on, nothing is sent until you tap its button.

Limits

Ten queries per day per device (one hundred with the PRO subscription), with the counter visible on screen. Abuse protection is provided by Firebase App Check.

9. The PRO subscription

VIDA RPG has an optional subscription, PRO, whose only effect is to raise the AI Coach's daily limit. It does not unlock the game, does not remove ads (there are none) and does not sell Baros or cosmetics, which are earned only by playing.

  • Payment is handled by your phone's store (Google Play or the App Store). The app does not see, receive or store your payment method.
  • The party that checks whether the subscription is still active is RevenueCat, a subscription intermediary. It receives the receipt issued by the store, the platform, the store country and an anonymous identifier it generates itself. It does not receive your name, your game, photographs or anything you send to the AI Coach.
  • If you do not buy and do not open the subscription screen, the app never connects to RevenueCat at all.
  • Your phone only stores whether you are PRO, which plan, and until when. That data is not included in the exported game file.
  • Subscriptions are cancelled from the store itself, like any other.

RevenueCat's, Google's and Apple's handling of this data is governed by their own privacy policies.

10. Home-screen widget (Android)

If you place the widget, the app keeps in its own private storage just enough to draw it: the day's rings, your streak count and a small image of your character in classic mode (never the photo-based version). It requests no permissions, uses no connection and never leaves the device.

11. Permissions

  • Notifications: only if you enable reminders. You can deny it.
  • Camera and photo access: only to create the avatar from a photo, requested at that moment, and you can deny them. The camera is declared as not required.
  • Internet: declared by the engine the app runs on. All resources are bundled; the only possible network requests are those of the AI Coach and the subscription, and only when you trigger them.
  • Vibration, plus the technical permissions the system uses to reschedule reminders after a reboot and to open the store's purchase sheet. None of them ask for your approval or access your data.

The app does not request the microphone, location, contacts, cross-app tracking, external storage writing or exact alarms, on either platform.

12. Your rights (GDPR)

The European Union's General Data Protection Regulation grants you the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection over your personal data. In VIDA RPG the developer does not collect or keep personal data on any server of their own, so there is nothing of yours to give access to, correct or delete. Those rights are exercised on your own device:

  • Access and portability: the export option in Settings gives you all your data in a readable file.
  • Rectification: everything you wrote can be edited inside the app.
  • Erasure: the "Reset the game" button in Settings erases all progress, and uninstalling the app deletes all data.

For any privacy enquiry, write to apds2012@gmail.com. If you believe your rights are not being respected, you may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or to the supervisory authority in your country.

13. Children

The app is not directed at children under thirteen and does not collect data from them. Since it collects no identifying data from anyone, it cannot collect any from children either. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has used the app, remember that all of their data is on the device and is removed by uninstalling it.

14. Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling VIDA RPG deletes all game data. The developer holds no copy whatsoever, so there is nothing else to delete or to request. The only remaining files would be those you exported yourself and saved wherever you chose.

15. Changes to this policy

If data handling changes in the future, this policy will be updated, published at this same address and shown inside the app (More → Privacy), in all three languages.
