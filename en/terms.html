1. What VIDA RPG is

VIDA RPG is a gamified personal-organisation tool. It turns your habits, vices, missions and goals into video-game mechanics: health points, experience, levels, a virtual currency called Baros, streaks, milestones, mentors and an optional AI Coach. Its purpose is to help you organise yourself and stay motivated, nothing more.

The rank compares what you log in the app with a reference model built from public statistics and with a scale of profiles set by the game. It does not measure your personal worth, your income or your health, and it is not information about other people.

8. Query credits

Since version 2.16.0 you can buy, if you want to, query credits, in packs of 5 and 20, through Google Play. A credit does the same as a rewarded ad, without watching it: one more AI Coach query, a new Feat from the third on or, since 2.17.0, one more encounter in the adventure; and, since 2.17.0, a credit also serves to unlock a piece of the original soundtrack for good, one per credit (never with Baros). Credits are not in-game currency: they are not earned by playing, give no EXP or Baros, do not turn into Baros, have no value outside the app and cannot be passed to anyone else. The balance is capped at 100 credits and is kept on the Coach server with an anonymous identifier, as explained in the Privacy Policy. They are not sold in Russia.

Before you pay, the app asks for two express consents, and without them you cannot buy: that you want the Coach to answer immediately, without waiting for the 14-day withdrawal period to end, and that you understand that, once the query has been provided, you lose your right of withdrawal (Article 16(m) of Directive 2011/83/EU; Articles 103(m) and 98(7) of the Spanish consolidated consumer protection law, TRLGDCU). After the purchase, the app gives you a receipt with the date, the product, the final price including VAT, the order number and the consent you gave; you will find it in Settings → Query credits.

Refund of unspent credits. Credits you have not spent can be refunded within 14 days of the purchase. Queries already made cannot be refunded, because they were provided with your consent. To request it, write to vidarpgapp@gmail.com with the order number from your receipt. The amount for the unspent credits is refunded through Google Play and those credits are removed from your balance. Google Play also handles refunds within 48 hours of the purchase, from your order history.

Recovering the balance and retention. If you reinstall the app, you can recover the credits you have not spent in Settings → Query credits → "Recover my credits", with the Google Play order number shown on your receipt; each order can be used for a recovery only once. Unspent credits that remain on the Coach server, and the pieces of music unlocked with credits, which are recovered along with them, are deleted after 24 months without activity (no purchases, no credits spent and no recoveries), or earlier if you ask for their deletion, and in both cases they are lost. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

9. AI Coach

The AI Coach is optional and runs on a developer server on Cloudflare, which queries an open language model through Workers AI (since 2.16.0, Gemma, by Google, run by Cloudflare and not by Google), with the limitations described in the Privacy Policy. You can switch it off completely from Settings. It has a query limit that depends on your plan (without paying, two a month; with PRO, one a day; each extra query, with a voluntary rewarded ad or a query credit) and that may change in future versions. It runs on a free daily quota shared by the whole community: if it runs out on a given day, the coach will not be available until the next day, and neither the query nor the credit is counted against you.

10. Rewarded ads

Since version 2.15.0 the app shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob only when you ask to watch one, in three places: for an extra AI Coach query, to open a new Feat from the third on without PRO (at most one a week) and, since 2.17.0, for one more encounter in the adventure once the new encounters available have been used up (at most two a day). An ad gives no EXP or Baros. If you close the ad before it finishes, there is no reward. Whether ads are available depends on Google and on your region (there are none in Russia), and there may be none at a given moment; PRO does not need them. In all three places a query credit can be used instead of the ad (section 8).
