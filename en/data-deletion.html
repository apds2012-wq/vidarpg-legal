Data Deletion Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · app version 2.20.0

Deleting your VIDA RPG data is simple, because almost all of it is on your device and nowhere else. There are two exceptions: what you share if you turn on Friends, since 2.20.0 (section 4), and the query credit balance, if you buy any, since 2.16.0 (section 6).

1. There are no accounts

VIDA RPG has no user accounts, no registration, no email, no phone number and no passwords. There is nothing to close. If you turn on Friends, your phone creates a random identifier and key, which are not an account: they carry none of your data and are deleted as explained in section 4. If you do not turn it on, nothing is ever created on any server on this account. The developer has no way to link a game to a person.

2. Where your data is

Your entire game (character, avatar, habits, vices, missions, goals, texts, progress, preferences) lives exclusively in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to any server and there is no VIDA RPG cloud copy. Only if you turn on Friends (since 2.20.0), a small, bounded part, your card and the "yes" entries of your shared challenges, is also kept on a developer server; section 4 explains which part and how it is deleted.

3. How to erase your progress from inside the app

  1. Open VIDA RPG and go to More → Settings.
  2. Tap "Reset the game".
  3. Tap again to confirm. The app asks twice because the game cannot be recovered.

On confirmation, all progress is erased: character, avatar (including the skin and hair tones taken from a photo, if you created it that way), habits, vices, missions, goals, texts, suns, experience, streak, records, achievements and history. Scheduled reminders are cancelled. If you have Friends turned on, in step 2 the app additionally asks "Also delete your Friends data?", with "Yes" selected by default. With "Yes", before resetting it deletes your card, your connections with your friends and your "yes" entries in challenges from the Friends server, the same as "Delete all my data" (section 4), and deletes the Friends identifier and key from the phone; if there is no connection, it does not reset the game and tells you so. With "No", it resets as before and your Friends data stays on the server until 90 days of non-use pass. If you do not have Friends turned on, the question does not appear and nothing changes. Only your chosen language is kept, so you are not asked again, together with a few technical markers stored outside the game (the AI Coach and purchase identifiers, the Friends identifier and key if you answered "No", the access code markers, a copy of the colour palette and whether you have already been asked for a rating; section 3 of the Privacy Policy), which are erased when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

If you only want to erase what you have written for your guide, in the techniques' opening questions and in the exercises of the coach's path, without losing your game, use Settings → "Forget my answers". The answers about what was going on around a slip are erased with "Reset the game".

4. Deleting what you share in Friends (since 2.20.0)

Only if you turned on Friends, the Friends server, on Cloudflare, keeps your card (the name you set, your avatar, your level, your title, your total EXP and your level aura), your connections with your friends and those you have blocked, and your "yes" entries for each day of the shared challenges, with your anonymous RevenueCat purchase identifier inside the challenge. It never keeps your habits, your slips, your health, your purchases or anything from Health Connect. It is all explained in section 12 bis of the Privacy Policy.

If you no longer have the app or you lost your phone, you cannot use "Delete all my data", because the key was on that phone (uninstalling the app also deletes it). You have two options: wait, and your card and your connections are deleted automatically after 90 days of non-use; or write to vidarpgapp@gmail.com with the name your card had and your four symbols (the ones the app shows in Friends › "Privacy and deletion"). Since there are no accounts, the developer only deletes your data if that name and those symbols match a single identity; if your data cannot be identified with certainty, it expires after 90 days of non-use. For this, the server keeps only a digest (fingerprint) of "name + symbols", never the name separately, and deletes it along with your card. Handling your request is free of charge and we reply within 15 days at most.

If you are the parent or guardian of a minor who turned on Friends (which is only for people aged 18 and over), you can ask for deletion the same way: write to vidarpgapp@gmail.com with the name of their card and their four symbols (section 16 of the Privacy Policy).

What cannot be deleted: screenshots that another person has taken, and what your friends had already seen before you stopped sharing. Your card disappears from each friend's list the next time their phone connects. Before uninstalling the app or changing phones, use "Delete all my data": uninstalling deletes your key and afterwards you could no longer do it from the app.

5. Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling VIDA RPG permanently deletes all game information from the device. Your game is not on any server: no copy of it remains with the developer, no retention period and no residual data from it. On Android, system backup is also disabled for this app. There are two exceptions: if you bought query credits, their balance stays on the Coach server, tied to an anonymous identifier, until you ask for its deletion or 24 months pass without activity (section 6); and if you used Friends, what is on its server stays until you delete it with "Delete all my data" before uninstalling or 90 days pass without use (section 4).

6. What is outside our reach

  • Files you exported. If you used "Export", the file is wherever you saved it. Delete it yourself if you want it gone.
  • iCloud backup (iPhone). If enabled, your game may be part of your own device's backup, inside your Apple account. It is managed from the iCloud settings.
  • AI Coach. Since 2.15.0, the coach server on Cloudflare does not store or log your queries: all it keeps, for the current day only, is a counter linked to a digest (fingerprint) of the random installation ID and another of the IP address, salted with the date, which are replaced the next day. The IP fingerprint is not irreversible: from it, an IPv4 address could be recovered by trying them all; that is why it is kept for that day only. Queries made up to 2.14 were processed by Google's services through Firebase, without any data that identifies you, under Google's terms. The developer keeps none of those queries.
  • Ads. If you asked to watch an ad, what Google AdMob collected to show it is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can change your consent in Settings → Ads → Ad privacy, and reset or delete the advertising ID from Android's settings.
  • PRO subscription. It is cancelled from Google Play or the App Store, like any subscription. The purchase record belongs to the store and to RevenueCat, under an anonymous identifier, under their policies.
  • Query credits. Since 2.16.0, if you buy query credits, the Coach server on Cloudflare keeps their balance, the order number of each purchase with its product, its date and whether it was refunded, the last day with activity and a short list of the latest uses, tied to the anonymous identifier that RevenueCat generates, not to your name. Resetting the game or uninstalling the app does not delete them. You can ask for them to be deleted: write to vidarpgapp@gmail.com with the order number from your receipt; any credits you have not spent are lost when they are deleted. If you do not ask, they are deleted automatically after 24 months without activity (no purchases, no credits spent and no recoveries). If you have only reinstalled the app and want to keep the balance, there is no need to delete anything: it can be recovered in Settings → Query credits → "Recover my credits", with the order number. Since 2.17.0, that server also keeps which soundtrack pieces you have unlocked with credits: they come back with the balance when you recover it, and they are deleted with the rest, on request or after 24 months without activity, which means they are lost as well.
  • Friends. Since 2.20.0, what the Friends server keeps and how it is deleted is in section 4. Uninstalling the app does not delete it, nor does resetting the game if you answer "No" to "Also delete your Friends data?"; "Delete all my data" does, as do "Reset the game" with "Yes" (section 3) and the passing of 90 days of non-use.
  • Access codes. Since 2.17.1, if you redeem a code, the Coach's server does not store the code, your ID or your IP: only, for that day, a digest (fingerprint) of the IP and another of the installation ID to limit attempts, deleted the next day, and, for the trial code, how many times it has been redeemed in total, with no data about who. Nothing of yours is left on the server to delete. The grant is stored in your game and is erased with it; the date of the first trial and the latest time seen are kept on the phone, outside the game: resetting the game does not erase them, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data does.

7. Contact

If you have any question about data deletion, or want to ask for Friends data to be deleted without the app, write to vidarpgapp@gmail.com.
