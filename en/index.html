VIDA ♥ RPG

Real life is the video game. Your character has 1000 health points: habits earn experience and Baros, vices cost health, and if it hits zero the only way back is completing a penalty. You move along an avenue, one block per level, with missions, mentors, an action plan and an optional AI Coach.

In one sentence

VIDA RPG has no user accounts, shows no ads and carries no trackers. Your whole game lives on your device. The only two features that connect to the internet, the AI Coach and the PRO subscription, are optional and only act when you ask them to.
