Instagram account privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy covers only the Instagram account @vidarpg and the internal tool it uses to publish its Reels and reply to comments ("VIDA RPG Promo"). The VIDA RPG app has its own privacy policy.

1. Controller

Alex Vida, independent developer, Spain. Contact: vidarpgapp@gmail.com.

2. What data we process and why

  • Comments with the Reel's keyword. If you comment on a @vidarpg Reel with the keyword shown in the video (for example "GAME"), Instagram sends us the comment text, its ID and your Instagram-scoped user ID for this tool. We use them only to send you the VIDA RPG Google Play link once by private message and to post a short public reply to your comment.
  • To avoid messaging you twice we keep your Instagram-scoped user ID together with the comment ID for at most 30 days; it is then deleted automatically. The comment text is not stored.
  • Insights of our own Reels (views, reach, average watch time, saves, shares): aggregated figures Instagram provides about @vidarpg's own posts, with no data about specific people.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering people who ask for the link by commenting, and your own request when you comment the keyword.

3. What we do not do

We do not use this data for advertising, we do not build profiles, we do not sell or share it with anyone, and we never message you unless you commented the keyword. Data is processed on Cloudflare (the tool's server) and Meta (Instagram) under their own terms.

4. Your rights and how to request deletion

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its use, by writing to vidarpgapp@gmail.com with your Instagram username. We delete it within 30 days at most (and it is deleted automatically after 30 days anyway). You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).
